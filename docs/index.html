NOTES FROM THE DESKTOP

A field guide
to finding things.

A good search starts with the right question. Here is how filename search, document indexing, and OCR fit together, without the jargon.

SEARCH-IN JOURNAL / 01THE EVERYDAY SEARCH

Desktop search is a way to find information in files on your own computer. The useful part is not just getting a list. It is knowing which result deserves your attention.

01

Start with what you remember.

If you remember a name, extension, or folder, start in Files. A filename search answers, "Where is that file?" It can locate a PDF by its name without reading the PDF's contents.

If you remember the words in the document instead, use In-File. Content search answers, "Which indexed document contains this text?" A distinctive phrase is often more useful than a common word that appears in hundreds of files.

NAME YOU REMEMBERAtlas-reportWORDS YOU REMEMBERdelivery approved
02

Give your search a place to begin.

When you know the project or folder, narrow the directory scope. Searching the right location can make a busy result list much easier to read. Use a file-type filter when you know you need a PDF, a picture, or another specific format.

The filename catalog and the document-content index have different scopes. Finding a filename does not prove its contents have been indexed. For In-File, check that the document is inside your chosen Content Indexer folders and that local extraction has finished.

03

A scan is a picture until its words are read.

A PDF can contain selectable text, scanned page images, or both. Searching a filename cannot reveal the words in an image. OCR, or optical character recognition, is the step that turns visible lettering into searchable text.

SEARCH-IN extracts available document text locally first. Eligible files that still need text can enter AI Pending. Optional AI OCR is a separate choice: configure a supported vision provider, review what needs processing, and approve the run. Provider fees and privacy terms are separate from the free desktop app.

04

Read the result before opening another app.

A Match snippet gives you a little context, not the whole story. Check the filename, path, and available file details, then use the embedded preview to see whether it is the right document. Where rendered text is available, move between visible matches or browse the relevant pages.

A preview is for quick inspection. Complex document layouts and specialist formats may look different from their native editor. When exact formatting matters, open the original file in its normal application.

Does SEARCH-IN search the web or my computer?

SEARCH-IN is desktop search software for your local files. Files searches names and paths; In-File searches text prepared by the Content Indexer. It is not a web search engine or a browser extension.

One helpful distinction: optional AI OCR reads eligible files you approve. It does not mean every search is sent to an AI model, or that a whole-PC search is an unrestricted AI chat with all your files.

A useful preview, with honest limits.

Complex Word layout is approximate. Excel previews show stored worksheet values, not a complete Excel environment. PowerPoint previews show basic slide text, not full presentations. Scans need OCR for text searching. Media codecs vary between PCs.

RAW, HEIC, TIFF, EPS, MOBI/AZW, and legacy Office renderers are not bundled. Installed Windows preview handlers may offer additional compatibility, but the full QuickLook plugin ecosystem is not included.

YOUR DESKTOP IS YOUR SPACE

Useful search.
Clear boundaries.

You should know where the work happens, what is optional, and what this public project does and does not contain.

Local by default.

Filename search, local content extraction, and embedded previews run on your computer. They do not require an AI subscription.

Uploads are a choice.

Optional AI OCR sends eligible files to the provider only after you choose that provider and approve the run.

Credentials stay private.

API credentials are protected for your Windows account and kept outside the installation folder. Releases do not contain your keys or local databases.

Free, not open source.

The public repository distributes releases, documentation, and demos. Application and engine source code remain private.

This landing page has no sign-in or analytics scripts. Demo recordings show synthetic documents and fictional paths, not personal files or working API credentials.

A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Before you dive in.

Do I need to keep the Content Indexer open to search?

You need the engine running to use its search catalog. The Content Indexer prepares document text; its window does not need to stay open just to search content that has already been indexed.

Why can Files find a document but In-File cannot find its words?

Files searches names and paths, while In-File searches indexed text. Check that the document is inside your content-index scope and has finished indexing. If it is a scan or its text cannot be read locally, review AI Pending and consider optional OCR.

Is SEARCH-IN really free?

Yes, SEARCH-IN is free to use. It is a closed-source application, not an open-source project. Optional third-party AI providers may charge for their services or apply usage limits.

Do I need Everything, EverythingToolbar, or QuickLook?

No. SEARCH-IN runs independently with its included engine. Install the required Microsoft desktop runtimes for the interface and embedded preview, not another search or preview application.

Why might a preview look different from the original app?

The preview is for quick inspection, not a replacement for every native editor. Advanced Word layouts, Excel formulas/charts, PowerPoint drawings, specialist formats, and media codecs have limits. Open the source file in its original app when full fidelity is essential.

What happens to an older LOCALOCR installation?

LOCALOCR 1.x remains separate. Installing SEARCH-IN does not silently convert or delete its databases or credentials. Earlier local SEARCH-IN builds with online updates disabled need one manual installation of 2.1.0.103.

Will an update interrupt me?

Available SEARCH-IN updates are announced by a quiet download indicator. Click it to review and download inside the app. You decide when to launch Setup; the website redesign does not change the application update feeds.
